## Production Sequence (10 sessions)

### Phase 1 — FOUNDATION (Sessions 1-3)

These documents feed everything else. Order matters.

**Session 1: `character_bible.md`**
- Deep profiles: backstory, psychology, speech patterns, arc per season
- All characters: Maciej, Cross, Ola, Priya, Krawczyk, Priya,
- WHY FIRST: Every outline, every pitch document, every screenplay draws on character depth. Without this, outlines will be surface-level.
- Depends on: series bible, beat sheet
- Feeds: everything downstream

**Session 2: `outlines_ep02-05.md`**
- Detailed outlines (3-5 pages each) for episodes 2-5 (first half of season)
- Each outline: scene-by-scene breakdown, key dialogue moments, act breaks, character beats
- WHY NOW: First half of season must be bulletproof — this is what a development executive reads to decide if the show has legs
- Depends on: character bible, beat sheet
- Feeds: pitch package, finale screenplay

**Session 3: `outlines_ep06-09.md`**
- Detailed outlines for episodes 6-9 (second half, pre-finale)
- Includes: R equation formalization (ep 6), Nobel ceremony (ep 8), AI experiment (ep 9)
- These are the episodes with the highest conceptual density — need precision
- Depends on: ep 2-5 outlines (continuity), character bible, R equation reference
- Feeds: finale screenplay, pitch package

---

### Phase 2 — CAPSTONE (Sessions 4-5)

The two documents that prove the season works as a complete story.

**Session 4: `ep10_finale_screenplay.md`**
- Full 60-page screenplay for "The Measurement Problem"
- The phone call. The convergence. R = ?
- WHY HERE: By now we have character bible + all 9 outlines. The finale can pay off every thread.
- Depends on: all outlines, character bible, R equation reference, AI reference
- Feeds: pitch package (proves season closes with power)

**Session 5: `pilot_rewrite_notes.md`**
- Detailed rewrite pass notes for the pilot
- Target: dialogue tightening, "as you know Bob" removal, subtext strengthening
- Line-by-line notes, not a full rewrite (rewrite itself is a separate task)
- WHY HERE: After writing 9 outlines + finale, we have much deeper understanding of characters. Rewrite notes will be sharper.
- Depends on: character bible, outlines (character voice consistency)
- Feeds: final pilot draft (future session)

---

### Phase 3 — PITCH PACKAGE (Sessions 6-8)

The documents that go in the envelope to HBO.

**Session 6: `one_pager.md`**
- Single page: logline, hook, format, comps, why now, why HBO
- Two variants: standalone version
- This is the FIRST thing anyone reads. It must be perfect.
- WHY HERE: After all the deep work, distilling to one page is possible. Before the deep work, it would be hollow.
- Depends on: everything above
- Feeds: first contact with producers/agents

**Session 7: `series_synopsis.md`**
- 2-3 pages: full series arc S1-S4
- Season-by-season overview with key turning points
- Includes: how Cross integration scales, where the science goes, thematic evolution
- Must read like a page-turner — executive should WANT to read the pilot after this
- Depends on: one-pager (tone/positioning), beat sheet, outlines
- Feeds: pitch meetings

**Session 8: `tone_and_comps.md`**
- Visual references, comparable shows, target audience, market positioning
- Mood board descriptions (can't include actual images, but describes them precisely)
- Comp analysis: Ted Lasso meets Arrival
- Includes: why HBO specifically, what HBO audience wants that this delivers
- Depends on: one-pager, synopsis
- Feeds: pitch meetings, producer conversations

---

### Phase 4 — DEPTH (Sessions 9-10)

Documents that demonstrate the show is real, not just clever.

**Session 9: `science_bible.md`**
- Real scientific references behind every RFC element
- IIT (Tononi), zero-point field (Keppler), E1P (Leescu), First Signal Law (Mureddu)
- How real science maps to show science — what's real, what's extrapolated, what's fictional
- Includes: consultant recommendations (real physicists who could advise)
- WHY IMPORTANT: This is what separates The Quantum Theory from every other sci-fi pitch. The science is real. This document proves it.
- Depends on: R equation reference, AI reference, outlines
- Feeds: credibility in pitch, potential science advisor attachment

**Session 10: `production_overview.md`**
- Practical production information
- Locations: Warsaw (Mokotów apartment, University, streets), Stockholm (Nobel ceremony), Boston/Cambridge (MIT), Stanford / Palo Alto
- Budget tier: mid-range prestige drama (no VFX, character-driven)
- Casting notes: Maciej Musiał rationale, Cross flexibility ( scheduling)
- Language strategy: English primary, Polish subtitled scenes
- Music: guitar motif, European vs. American sonic palette
- Depends on: everything (this is the "how we actually make it" document)
- Feeds: producer conversations, budget discussions

---

## Dependencies Warning

If any session is skipped or reordered:

- Skipping Session 1 (character bible) weakens EVERYTHING downstream
- Sessions 2-3 (outlines) must come before Session 4 (finale) — non-negotiable
- Sessions 6-8 (pitch) should come after Sessions 1-5 — distilling before deep work = hollow pitch
- Sessions 9-10 are flexible in order but should come last

---

## One Rule for Every Session

Start by reading the relevant existing documents. Don't write from memory — write from the established foundation. Consistency across 15 documents is what makes a pitch package feel like a SHOW, not a collection of ideas.
