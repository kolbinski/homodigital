# Privacy Policy — Homo Digital

**Last updated: June 9, 2026**

## 1. Who we are

Homo Digital is a career agent platform for senior software engineers. The platform is operated by Krzysztof Olbiński, based in Poland. Contact: contact@homodigital.io

## 2. What data we collect

**Account data:**
- Email address
- First and last name
- Phone number (optional)

**Professional profile:**
- Work experience, skills, education
- Salary preferences and career goals
- CV data used for job applications

**Usage data:**
- Job applications submitted on your behalf
- Offers viewed and their statuses
- Push notification tokens (for sending you updates)

**Technical data:**
- Device type and operating system
- App usage timestamps

## 3. Why we collect it

We use your data exclusively to:
- Match job offers to your profile using AI
- Apply to job offers on your behalf
- Send you push notifications about new matches and applications
- Communicate with you about your job search

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Anthropic Claude API** — for AI-powered offer matching and CV generation
- **Supabase** — database hosting (EU region)
- **Railway** — API hosting
- **Expo / Google Firebase** — push notification delivery

All third-party providers process data under their own privacy policies and applicable data protection law.

## 5. How long we keep it

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by emailing contact@homodigital.io. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request.

## 6. Your rights (GDPR)

As a user in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to processing
- Request data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@homodigital.io.

## 7. Push notifications

We send push notifications about:
- New job offers matched to your profile
- Applications submitted on your behalf
- Sync reports with new matches

You can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings.

## 8. Security

Your data is stored in encrypted databases. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only. We use HTTPS for all data transmission.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.
