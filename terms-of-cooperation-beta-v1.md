## 2. What is the beta programme

The beta programme is a test phase of the "personal career agent" service provided by Homo Digital. As one of the first 3 clients, the Participant receives access to the service in exchange for active cooperation and honest feedback.

---

## 3. What we offer

As part of the monthly subscription, the agent:

- actively searches for job opportunities matched to the Participant's profile
- builds or optimises the Participant's LinkedIn profile
- prepares the Participant for job interviews
- contacts recruiters on behalf of the Participant
- maintains a live application report in Google Sheets accessible to the Participant in real time
- remains available by phone or WhatsApp

At the end of each month the Participant receives a report on actions taken.

---

## 4. What we do NOT guarantee

The beta programme does not guarantee:

- finding a job within a specific timeframe
- a minimum number of opportunities or interviews
- a specific salary increase

The Service Provider makes every effort to deliver the most effective service possible, however results depend on many factors outside the agent's control (market conditions, the Participant's profile, employer decisions).

## 5. Fees

**Monthly subscription:** PLN 150 gross — payable in advance by bank transfer against an invoice issued by the Service Provider.

**Success fee:** a one-off payment upon changing jobs — equal to 50% of the Participant's net monthly salary increase. Payable within 14 days of starting the new role.

_Example: if the Participant changed jobs and earns PLN 3,000 more per month — the success fee is PLN 1,500._

The success fee applies only when the job change occurred during an active subscription or within 30 days of its termination, and the job offer originated from or was handled by the agent.

Cooperation begins on the date the first payment is received and lasts one calendar month.

Either party may terminate cooperation at the end of the current month by notifying the other party at least 7 days before the end of the billing period. The subscription paid is non-refundable for a month already started.
