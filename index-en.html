What your career agent actually does

🔍 LinkedIn & CV Optimization

Your career agent builds a LinkedIn profile that attracts recruiters and rewrites your CV in the language of results — achiever, not doer. This service alone costs as much as a monthly subscription elsewhere.

📋 Active Job Search & Outreach

Your agent reviews dozens of offers weekly, sends personalized CVs for each application after your approval, and reaches out directly to recruiters and hiring managers at target companies.

📞 Employer Screening Before You

Before your interview — your career agent talks to the employer first. Checks company culture, catches red flags (e.g. micromanagement, no code review), asks if salary is flexible upward. You get a full brief.

💼 Interview Preparation

Before every interview you get a briefing: company research, typical technical questions, how to talk about your previous departure. Salary negotiations are handled by your agent — you focus on the technical conversation.

💰 Salary Negotiation

Your career agent conducts financial negotiations directly with the employer — you focus on the technical conversation, your agent talks about money. No emotions, with arguments and market benchmarks.

📈 Long-term Career Development

Your agent doesn't disappear when you find a job. They identify which skills, certifications and tech trends — including AI and vibe-coding — will increase your market value and make sure you don't wake up in the same situation a year from now.

📊 Live Tracker & Monthly Report

You see every step in real time — live tracker in Google Sheets. At the end of each month you get a full report: how many offers reviewed, how many rejected and why, what was applied to and the plan for next month.

💬 WhatsApp Access

You have direct access to your career agent via WhatsApp. Response within 24 hours — no bots, no forms, no tickets.
