What your agent does

01

Finds opportunities and applies for you

Not just through job boards. Your agent uses their network, direct outreach and AI tools to find opportunities you'd never see — and applies on your behalf, personalising every application for each specific company.

02

Negotiates for you

Most developers leave money on the table out of fear of negotiating. Your agent calls the recruiter instead of you — just like an actor's agent talks to the studio.

03

Prepares you for interviews

Before every interview, your agent knows what to highlight, what to downplay and how to present your experience for that specific company.

04

Builds your personal brand

Actively manages your LinkedIn profile, suggests what and when to publish, monitors your network. You're visible before you even start looking.

05

Thinks long-term

Not just the next job — your entire career trajectory. Courses, certifications, timing the move. Someone who sees the bigger picture when you're too close to it.

06

Reports on everything done

Every month you receive a report — what was done, which opportunities were found, what actions were taken. You always know someone is working for you.

How it works

1

You create a profile

You describe your experience, career goals, preferences and expectations. The platform matches you with an agent with the right specialisation and market knowledge.

2

You meet your agent

You talk with your agent by phone or WhatsApp. Together you set priorities, strategy and way of working. No chatbot — a real person.

3

Your agent acts on your behalf

Finds opportunities, contacts recruiters, builds your visibility. You focus on work — your agent handles the rest.

4

You land a better job

When the right opportunity appears, your agent guides you through the entire process and negotiates your salary. You pay a success fee only when the move happens.
